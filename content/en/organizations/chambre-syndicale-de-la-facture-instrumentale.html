Since 1890, the Chambre Syndicale de la Facture Instrumentale has brought together manufacturers and craftspeople who make, repair, restore, distribute and export musical instruments and related accessories. It also includes retailers, five professional associations of instrument makers (Aladfi, Glaaf, Aplg, Unfi, Uffip), the trade association for retailers (CSMM) and the professional organisation for piano technicians (Europiano France)...
